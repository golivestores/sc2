OUR SOLUTIONS · MGA-1
The MGA-1 measures CO₂ and H₂O adsorption using two sensors: a gravimetric sensor for total uptake and a capacitance sensor for polar molecule (e.g. H₂O) uptake.
Test methods include isotherms, adsorption time constants, and thermal cycling for aging and stability.
Compact sensors and flow cells accelerate test times up to 20× faster than other techniques.
